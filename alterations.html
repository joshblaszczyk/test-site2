Before you buy

Our purchase & re-consignment policy.

At ThomKat, we want you to love your find! Please review our policy before completing your purchase.

  • All sales are final. Refunds are not available.
  • Once purchased, a gown becomes the property of the buyer.
  • If you decide to part with your gown, you may be eligible to re-consign it with ThomKat. Under our re-consignment policy, you receive 50% of the final selling price when the item sells, subject to our acceptance and consignment terms.

Alterations & Tailoring

Whether you need a few alterations, want to preserve a family heirloom, or are searching for a special garment, ThomKat Fashion and Art Boutique is here to help make your vision a reality.

From formal gowns and bridal wear to jeans, tops, and everyday favorites, we offer alteration services to help you achieve the fit you desire. We also welcome outside items, so you don't have to purchase your garment from us to take advantage of our services.

Every Fitting, Made Personal
