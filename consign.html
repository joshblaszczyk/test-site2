How it works

Snap it. Check it.
Bring it in.

Consigning with ThomKat starts right here on your phone.

Add photos of your piece and its tags below. The listing tool reads the label, suggests a title, category, size, and price, and lays it all out as a draft you can edit before you ever leave the house.

  • Snap —Photograph your piece

    Front, back, and a clear shot of the brand label and size tag.

  • Check —Review your draft

    Fix anything the tool missed: title, size, condition, and price.

  • Bring —Visit the boutique

    Call ahead or stop by 1 N. Main Street with your piece.

  • Sell —We take it from there

    We review your item and talk through pricing with you.

All consigned items are subject to our acceptance and consignment terms. Bought it from ThomKat? You may be eligible for our re-consignment policy: you receive 50% of the final selling price when it sells.
