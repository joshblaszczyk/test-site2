Before you buy

Our purchase & re-consignment policy.

At ThomKat, we want you to love your find! Please review our policy before completing your purchase.

  • All sales are final. Refunds are not available.
  • Once purchased, a gown becomes the property of the buyer.
  • If you decide to part with your gown, you may be eligible to re-consign it with ThomKat. Under our re-consignment policy, you receive 50% of the final selling price when the item sells, subject to our acceptance and consignment terms.
