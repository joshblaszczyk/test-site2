Our story

Why settle for ordinary?

Located in the heart of downtown Lombard, Illinois, ThomKat Fashion opened in 2021 with a vision to make dressing for special occasions fun, memorable, and affordable.

We specialize in an eclectic collection of hundreds of unique, vintage, and modern formal gowns available for both purchase and rental. With sizes ranging from 0 to 24 and options to accommodate a variety of budgets, we believe everyone deserves to feel confident and beautiful without overspending.

Why settle for ordinary when you can discover something extraordinary? From timeless elegance to vintage charm and modern glamour, our ever-changing collection offers something special for a variety of occasions.
