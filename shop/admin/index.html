Weekly uploads / Pending info
Pending info
Check each piece, fix anything that is wrong, drop in the photos (named by tag, like t1261100a.jpg, a is the main photo), then press the button. Nothing goes live until you do.


Weekly uploads / Products
Item search
Find an item
Search an item number, or a word like dresses, a brand or a color and pick the piece. In the box that opens, enter what it sold for (or press a discount, like 20% off), pick where it sold, then press Mark sold. That takes it off the website, and a consigned piece pays its consignor from what it really sold for.
Sold items & shipment tracking
Record when each product was mailed, its tracking number, and when it was delivered. Update these details manually.
Items stay here for 14 days after being marked sold, then move to the archive for 30 days.
Archive
Items are removed from the archive 30 days after entering the archive.


Weekly uploads / Sales
Weekly sales spreadsheet
Review products sold during a Monday–Sunday week, amounts and order progress.
Order statusSold dateTransactionItem / productLocationStore pickup / ShippedOriginal priceCustomer's cutStore commission (we made)Items in transaction
Enter an amount and an optional transaction number in a sold item's shipment details. Items with the same transaction number are counted as one order; items without one are counted separately. A transaction is complete only when all its items are marked complete. The customer's cut is the amount sold times the customer's split (40% or 50%), which you set in the same place.
