What is TLR Gives Back Foundation?

A Foundation Built on Gratitude, Purpose, and Giving Back

Growing up, my family had very little. Like many families facing difficult times, we relied on organizations such as The Salvation Army and People’s Resource Center for necessities like food and clothing. I will always be grateful for the help and kindness we received during those years. Today, I am blessed to be in a position where I can give back to those same organizations and help make a difference in my community.

For a long time, I struggled to find the purpose and direction for TLR Gives Back Foundation. One thing I knew for certain was that I wanted to help children and individuals in our community discover opportunities, pursue their dreams, and believe in what they could accomplish.

Then I thought about my grandmother, Mrs. Cleora Thomas Harris, and everything she meant to our family.

At the age of 82, my grandmother developed dementia and Alzheimer’s disease. It was heartbreaking for our family. We didn’t fully understand the disease or how it would affect her. One moment she might recognize you, and the next she might not remember who you were.

But there was one thing she never forgot: how to play the piano and organ.

Some of my most treasured memories are of my grandmother sitting at her instrument. We could name almost any song, and she would play it so beautifully, without missing a note. Even as her memory changed, her connection to music remained.

Those memories helped me discover the direction and purpose of TLR Gives Back Foundation. I realized that Music and the Arts would be at the heart of our mission.

Supporting Dreams Through Music & The Arts

We believe everyone deserves an opportunity to explore their creativity, develop their talents, and experience the joy of the Arts.

Through fundraising events, community partnerships, and generous contributions, TLR Gives Back Foundation provides scholarships and support to individuals and groups of all ages who want to participate in or pursue opportunities in Music and the Arts.

Our goal is to help keep music, dance, theatre, and other creative programs alive in our schools and communities, while encouraging the next generation to discover their own talents and possibilities.

The 2nd Chance Program — Everyone Deserves an Opportunity

Giving back also means believing in people, even when life hasn’t gone according to plan.

In addition to hosting fundraisers and special events, TLR Gives Back Foundation developed a partnership with the DuPage County Juvenile Detention Center and established our 2nd Chance Program.

The program is built on a simple belief: making a mistake should not define a person’s future. Everyone deserves an opportunity to learn, grow, and make better choices.

Through our program, young adults are welcomed into a fun, safe, and supportive learning environment where they can develop practical skills and build confidence.

Participants have opportunities to learn:

  • Photography for online selling
  • Creative writing for online selling
  • Marketing and product promotion
  • Visual merchandising and displays
  • Organization and inventory management
  • Other valuable skills that can help prepare them for future opportunities

We want participants to leave with more than community service experience. We want them to leave with new skills, a sense of accomplishment, and the confidence to see what is possible for their future.

Giving Back Through Business

We believe business can be a powerful way to make a positive difference. A portion of our online sales is donated to TLR Gives Back Foundation to help support the foundation’s continued growth, programs, and community outreach.

Every purchase, fundraiser, donation, and act of kindness helps us move closer to our goals.

At TLR Gives Back Foundation, it’s about more than raising money. It’s about creating opportunities, encouraging dreams, believing in second chances, and making a difference in the lives of others.

Because sometimes all it takes is one opportunity, one helping hand, or one beautiful note to change someone’s life.

Shopping with us.

We have quality in-season fashions to please our shoppers (sizes 0–22). They will take you from everyday life to a formal event at unbelievable prices. We pride ourselves on providing women with the “boutique” experience, which means a pleasant shopping experience, coupled with exceptional customer service, and the opportunity to assist you in looking like a million bucks without breaking the bank.

Let The Ladies Room provide you with the personal touch every shopper hopes to experience! We will have you wondering why you ever shopped traditional retail! Your satisfaction is our #1 concern.

Most of our clothing is pre-owned. All of it has been thoroughly inspected. Condition ranges from normal signs of wear to like new with the tags still on! We look over each piece and will describe anything that is out of the ordinary, but we have and will inevitably overlook things. If you have received your item(s) and find that there is a defect we have missed, please contact us as soon as possible at 224.474.4625.

Local buyers can pick up from the store at no additional cost. You would simply need to provide us with a pick-up date and time.
